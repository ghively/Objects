# Books

A **Book** keeps track of what you read, with the authors, the genre and a rating. It comes with "Start with basic setup"; its objects live in `Books/`.

## Create one

Press **New** (Ctrl+U) and pick **Book**, or right-click **Books** in the sidebar and choose **Create Book**. Use the book's title as the object's title.

## Properties

- **Authors**: links to people. Create the author as a Person first, then pick them here.
- **Genre**: one or more labels, from Adventure to Young Adult.
- **Type**: Fiction or Non-fiction.
- **Rating**: a number shown as a bar out of 5.
- **Reading timeline**: when you started and finished reading (a date range).
- **Cover image** and **Notes** (the body: quotes, highlights, what you thought).

## Tips

- An author's page lists every book that links to them.
- On the Books page, Sort and Filter work on all of these, for example every book rated 4 or more.
