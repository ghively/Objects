# People

A **Person** is a page for someone you know or work with. The type comes with "Start with basic setup" when you create a space; its objects live in the `People/` folder.

## Create one

Press **New** at the top of the sidebar (Ctrl+U), pick **Person**, and type the name. You can also right-click **People** in the sidebar and choose **Create Person**, or use **New** on the People page. The new person opens with the title ready to type.

## Properties

- **Picture**: the cover image at the top of the page. Hover the header and choose "Add cover image".
- **Category**: one or more labels, such as Friends, Family, Colleague or Author.
- **Organizations**: links to the places they belong to, of any type.
- **Projects** and **Meetings**: links to the projects and meetings they are part of.
- **Notes**: the page body. Write anything here.

## Tips

- Mention a person anywhere with `@` or `[[`. Their page lists every object that links to them, and names them in places that don't link yet (unlinked mentions).
- A meeting's **Attendees** and a project's **Collaborators** pick from your people.
