# People

A **Person** is a note for someone you know or work with. If your space doesn't have the type yet, add it with the `+` beside **Types** in the sidebar (People, under Use cases). Its notes live in the `People/` folder.

## Create one

Press Ctrl+U for the New menu, pick **Person**, and type the name. You can also right-click **People** in the sidebar and choose **Create Person**, or use **New Person** on the People page. The new person opens with the title ready to type.

## Properties

- **Picture**: the cover image at the top of the page. Hover the header and choose "Add cover image".
- **Category**: one or more labels, such as Friends, Family, Colleague or Author.
- **Organizations**: links to the places they belong to, of any type.
- **Projects** and **Meetings**: links to the projects and meetings they are part of.
- **Notes**: the text of the note. Write anything here.

## Tips

- Mention a person anywhere with `@` or `[[`. Their note lists every note that links to them, and the places that name them without a link yet (unlinked mentions).
- A meeting's **Attendees** and a project's **Collaborators** pick from your people.
