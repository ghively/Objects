# Meetings

A **Meeting** holds the notes from one meeting: who came, when, and what was said. If your space doesn't have the type yet, add it with the `+` beside **Types** in the sidebar (Meetings, under Use cases). Its notes live in `Meetings/`.

## Create one

Press Ctrl+U for the New menu and pick **Meeting**, or right-click **Meetings** in the sidebar and choose **Create Meeting**. Give it a title, set the date and time, and write the notes below.

## Properties

- **Date**: the day and time it starts. Add an end time for its length; without one it counts as 30 minutes.
- **Type**: Team Meeting, One-on-One, Client Meeting, Brainstorming, Workshop, Presentation, Interview or Conference.
- **Attendees**: links to the people who came.
- **Project**: the project it belongs to.
- **Reviewed**: tick it once you have gone through the notes.
- **Cover image** and **Notes** (the text).

## Tips

- A meeting shows in the Calendar on its **Date**. If you use the Work page, one with a time is also on its **Today** timeline for that day.
- Link the meeting from a person's or project's **Meetings** property, or link them from the meeting, to keep the trail in one place.
